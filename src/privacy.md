# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This policy covers the personal website at `illegalscreed.cn`, the Algorithm
Visualizer at `algo.illegalscreed.cn`, and the first-stage Type Pal game at
`pal.illegalscreed.cn`.

## Information processed

The personal website, Algorithm Visualizer, and Type Pal use three separate
Google Analytics 4 properties to understand aggregate page visits. The Google
Analytics script is not loaded unless you choose **Allow** in the site's
analytics notice. If allowed, Google Analytics may process the sanitized page
URL, browser and device characteristics, approximate location, referrer, and
cookies or similar identifiers according to Google's policies.

These sites send standard page-view events only. Page URLs sent to Analytics
exclude arbitrary query parameters and fragments; only validated campaign
parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content`) may
be retained. Searches, algorithm inputs, playback, quiz, sharing, and other
interactions are not sent as custom Analytics events. Type Pal also does not
send keyboard input, scenes, battles, game progress, saves, characters,
performance data, or errors as Analytics events.

Type Pal stores game saves in your browser using IndexedDB and may store
settings such as audio volume locally. This local game data is not included in
Analytics page-view events.

The Algorithm Visualizer is prepared to use Google AdSense. If advertising is
approved and enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or
similar identifiers to deliver, measure, and protect ads. The personal homepage
does not load the AdSense advertising script.

The web servers may temporarily process standard request logs, including IP
address, user agent, requested path, response status, and timestamp, for
security and operational troubleshooting. These three sites do not currently
offer account registration or comments.

## Advertising choices and consent

You can accept, decline, or later reopen **Privacy settings** on any of the
three sites.
Declining leaves Google Analytics unloaded. You can also review or change
Google's advertising personalization settings at
[Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also use the
[Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

Before personalized advertising is enabled where consent is required, including
the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the publisher will use a
Google-certified consent management platform through AdSense Privacy &
messaging.

## External services

Project links may take you to GitHub or other independently operated sites.
Their own privacy policies apply after you leave this domain.

## Contact

For a privacy question, open an issue in the
[personal site repository](https://github.com/IllegalCreed/IllegalCreed.github.io/issues).
Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
