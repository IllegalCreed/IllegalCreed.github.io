# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This policy covers the personal website at `illegalscreed.cn`, the Algorithm
Visualizer at `algo.illegalscreed.cn`, IllegalCreed Quiz at
`quiz.illegalscreed.cn`, and the first-stage Type Pal game at
`pal.illegalscreed.cn`.

## Information processed

The four products use four separate Google Analytics 4 properties to understand
aggregate page visits. IllegalCreed Quiz may also use a separate Baidu Analytics
property for traffic from China. These analytics scripts are not loaded unless
you choose **Allow** in the site's analytics notice. If allowed, the providers
may process the sanitized page URL, browser and device characteristics,
approximate location, referrer, and cookies or similar identifiers according to
their policies.

These products send standard page-view events only. Page URLs sent to Analytics
exclude arbitrary query parameters and fragments; only validated campaign
parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content`) may
be retained. Searches, algorithm inputs, playback, quiz, sharing, and other
interactions are not sent as custom Analytics events. Type Pal also does not
send keyboard input, scenes, battles, game progress, saves, characters,
performance data, or errors as Analytics events. IllegalCreed Quiz does not send
questions, selected answers, account identifiers, or category preferences to
Analytics. GA4 Enhanced Measurement is disabled for the Quiz property.

Type Pal stores game saves in your browser using IndexedDB and may store
settings such as audio volume locally. This local game data is not included in
Analytics page-view events.

IllegalCreed Quiz can be used as a guest or with an optional account. Account
records may contain a username, password hash, and an optional nickname or email.
Guest answers are not retained as answer history. For signed-in users, the
service stores question IDs, selected answers, correctness, timestamps, and
category preferences to provide history and learning-preference features.
Browser storage may hold a login token, guest category selection, theme,
prompts, and analytics choice.

While a Quiz tab is connected for announcements, the service temporarily keeps
a per-tab client ID, IP address, sign-in state, and current pathname in server
memory. Query parameters are not included, and the entry is removed when the
connection closes. General mutation logs may also contain answer submission and
result metadata, IP address, request path, success status, and timestamp for
security and operational troubleshooting. These logs are separate from the
signed-in answer-history feature.

The Algorithm Visualizer is prepared to use Google AdSense. If advertising is
approved and enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or
similar identifiers to deliver, measure, and protect ads. The personal homepage
does not load the AdSense advertising script. IllegalCreed Quiz does not load
the AdSense script while its advertising setup and certified consent path are
not active.

The web servers may temporarily process standard request logs, including IP
address, user agent, requested path, response status, and timestamp, for
security and operational troubleshooting. The personal site, Algorithm
Visualizer, and first-stage Type Pal do not currently offer account
registration or comments. IllegalCreed Quiz offers the optional account
described above.

## Advertising choices and consent

You can accept, decline, or later reopen **Privacy settings** on each product.
Declining leaves the applicable analytics providers unloaded. On IllegalCreed
Quiz and Type Pal, an unset choice accompanied by a DNT or GPC browser signal
defaults to declined unless you explicitly choose otherwise. You can also review or change
Google's advertising personalization settings at
[Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also use the
[Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

Before personalized advertising is enabled where consent is required, including
the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the publisher will use a
Google-certified consent management platform through AdSense Privacy &
messaging.

## External services

Their own privacy policies apply after you leave this domain.
